Before you order a site

Four questions that come up every time.

Ask your own
What do you need from me?

Your logo, texts or at least notes for them, photos, and prices. If the texts are not ready, we write them from your notes in one language; that is part of the price.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Yes. You get a one-page guide for the usual changes: prices, opening hours, photos. Bigger changes cost €40 an hour, agreed before we start.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?

You do, directly to the provider, so nothing depends on us. For a small site this is usually a few euros a month. We set it up with you.

When will my site show up in Google?

Google usually finds a new site within days or weeks after we submit it. Where it ranks takes months and is not something anyone controls. The setup removes the technical reasons to rank low.
