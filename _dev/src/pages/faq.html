{{I:shopping-bag}}Ordering

What’s the difference between a ready-made and a custom set? {{i:plus}}

A ready-made set is one of our 18 designs: you add your words and get every size right away. A custom set is designed for your brand and offer from scratch, starting with a free sketch.

How fast can you start? {{i:plus}}

Usually the same day. We reply to a brief within two working hours and send the first sketch within 48 hours after you say yes.

What is the free resize test? {{i:plus}}

Send one banner you already have. Within 24 working hours we send it back in three more sizes, free, with no obligation. It’s the quickest way to judge our work.

{{I:layers}}Sizes and files

Which sizes do I actually need? {{i:plus}}

For most campaigns, eight: six for Google (300 × 250, 336 × 280, 728 × 90, 300 × 600, 320 × 50, 970 × 250) and two for social (1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1920). See the size guide for all 26.

Will my files pass Google Ads? {{i:plus}}

Yes. Static images stay under 150 KB and HTML5 banners under 600 KB, the upload limits Google publishes. If a platform still rejects a file we made, we fix it free within 24 hours.

What file formats do I get? {{i:plus}}

PNG and JPG for every size, an HTML5 zip with a backup image if you ordered animation, print PDFs with CMYK colours and 3 mm bleed for print sizes, plus editable Figma and Canva files.

Do you print? {{i:plus}}

No. We prepare print-ready PDFs that any print house accepts, and we’re happy to talk to your printer about their requirements.

{{I:languages}}Languages and copy

Can you write in Estonian and Russian? {{i:plus}}

Yes, and English. Each version is written for its language, not translated word by word, and checked in the smallest size of your set.

What if my headline is too long? {{i:plus}}

We tell you before it becomes a problem and suggest a shorter version that fits 320 × 50. Long headlines are the most common reason small banners fail.

{{I:rotate-ccw}}Edits and guarantees

How many edits are included? {{i:plus}}

Two rounds in a custom set, three in a campaign. A round means one list of changes from you. Extra rounds are €40.

What if I don’t like the first sketch? {{i:plus}}

You pay nothing and keep the sketch. We’d rather lose a job than deliver something you don’t believe in.

What don’t you do? {{i:plus}}

Logos from scratch, photo shoots, hand-drawn characters, 3D product renders and buying ad space. We only sell what we can do perfectly, and point you to the right people for the rest.

{{I:key-round}}Licence

Can I use a ready-made set for several campaigns? {{i:plus}}

Yes. One licence covers one brand, any number of campaigns, with no time limit. You may not resell the template itself.

Who owns a custom set? {{i:plus}}

You do, once it’s paid: all files, sources and rights to use them. We may show it in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

{{I:receipt}}Payment

How do I pay? {{i:plus}}

By invoice, VAT included. Custom work: half after you approve the first sketch, half on delivery. Ready-made sets are paid at checkout.

Can I get a refund? {{i:plus}}

For ready-made sets: yes, within 14 days, if you haven’t downloaded the files. For custom work you only pay once you like the sketch.
