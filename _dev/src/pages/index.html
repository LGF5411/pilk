All questions {{i:arrow-right}}
Which sizes do I actually need? {{i:plus}}

Most campaigns do well with eight: 300 × 250, 336 × 280, 728 × 90, 300 × 600, 320 × 50 and 970 × 250 for Google, plus 1080 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920 for social. The brief form picks them for you.

Will my files pass Google Ads and Meta? {{i:plus}}

Yes. Static files stay under 150 KB and HTML5 under 600 KB, the Google Ads upload limits. If a platform still rejects a file we made, we fix it free within 24 hours.

Can I change the text later myself? {{i:plus}}

Yes. Every set comes with editable Figma and Canva files. Changing a price or a date takes about five minutes.

Do you write the text too? {{i:plus}}

If you want. We write headlines in English, Estonian and Russian, each checked in the smallest size of your set, with five variants for A/B tests.

What do you need from me? {{i:plus}}

Your logo, the offer, a link to your site and any photos you want used. Brand colours and fonts if you have them; if not, we suggest them.

How do I pay? {{i:plus}}

By invoice, VAT included. For custom work: half after you approve the free first sketch, half on delivery. Ready-made sets are paid at checkout.
